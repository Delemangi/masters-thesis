Term & Meaning in this thesis \\
\midrule
\endhead
API & Application programming interface. \\
Assistance & The combined outcome of a supported answer or useful routing (a clarification, verification step or referral). It is an AI-assessed endpoint, not verified factual accuracy. \\
BFF & Backend for frontend, the web application's server-side adapter. \\
Bootstrap & A way to get an interval by repeatedly resampling the questions and recomputing the result. \\
BM25 & A keyword ranking based on how often the query words appear in a passage. \\
CAS & Central Authentication Service, the faculty's single sign-on service. \\
CI & Confidence interval. The reported paired intervals are based on saved AI labels. \\
Clean and contaminated sets & A set is clean if no fix had been aimed at it before it was measured. It is contaminated once fixes were made for failures seen on it. \\
Component study & The evaluation with a local generator (Section~\ref{sec:component-study}). \\
Controlled configuration & The fixed retrieve-then-generate setup of the controlled experiments (Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}). \\
Decision model & A model that answers yes or no for each passage. Its probability is used as the score. \\
Fallback & Keeping the best candidate when neither the FAQ nor the document corpus has a candidate above its score floor. It is distinct from recovery after a reranker error. \\
FAQ & Frequently asked question and its stored answer. \\
FCSE & Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering. \\
Floor & The minimum reranker score a passage needs to enter the model's prompt. Threshold means the same thing. \\
G0/G1 & Policies with one shared FAQ/document score floor, with fallback off (G0) or on (G1). \\
\textsc{Gap} & A grade in the live evaluation: no confirmed fact covers the question and the answer is reasonable. \\
GPU & Graphics processing unit used for embedding, reranking or local inference. \\
Guard & See reference check. \\
Hedge & Vague wording that avoids stating a rule plainly. \\
Hit@$k$ & The share of questions where a relevant passage is among the top $k$ results. \\
HNSW & Hierarchical navigable small world graph, an approximate nearest-neighbor index. \\
Holdout & A set of questions kept apart from the fixes made to the system, so it can test the system fairly. \\
Kappa ($\kappa$) & Agreement between two raters corrected for chance. 0 is chance level and 1 is perfect agreement. \\
LLM & Large language model. \\
LQ1/LQ2 & The two questions about the deployed agent in Chapter~\ref{ch:introduction}. RQ1 to RQ3 are the questions about the controlled experiments. \\
Luna & GPT-5.6 Luna, the hosted model used for references, generation and primary judgments in the controlled experiments. The live evaluation used the newer GPT-6 Luna. \\
Material claim & An important statement about matters such as eligibility, fees, deadlines, procedure or an institutional action. \\
MCP & Model Context Protocol, used by the application for structured tool access. \\
MRR & Mean reciprocal rank, the average of 1 divided by the rank of the first relevant passage. \\
nDCG@10 & Normalized discounted cumulative gain over the top ten ranked candidates. Higher is better. \\
Opus & Claude Opus 5.5, the judge model of the earlier live-evaluation runs. \\
Production question & A question recorded by the deployed chatbot, as opposed to a historical email question. \\
Qwen & Qwen3-8B, a local model compressed to 4 bits and used as a second judge in the controlled experiments. \\
RAG & Retrieval-augmented generation. \\
Reference & The AI-produced description of required facts, evidence, action and uncertainty used for assessment. \\
Reference check & The automatic check that each quotation in a reference occurs in its named source. If it fails, a good label is downgraded to uncertain. Also called the guard. \\
Replay & Re-running the production retrieval and prompt logic on captured candidates, with controlled policy changes. \\
S0/S1 & Policies with separate FAQ and document score floors, with fallback off (S0) or on (S1). \\
Sign test & A paired test of whether gains and losses are balanced. A p-value near 1 means they are about equal. \\
Sonnet & Claude Sonnet 5.5, the judge model of all clean live-evaluation runs. It is also the app's default model for signed-in users, but it did not answer in the evaluations. \\
SSE & Server-sent events, a protocol for streaming responses over HTTP. \\
Stage & A numbered step in the development of the deployed agent (Stages 1 to 11 in Chapter~\ref{ch:live}). In the architecture, a stage is one part of the answering pipeline. \\
Tuned set & The set of emails that the fixes and confirmed facts were made from. It is also called the development set. \\
Wilson interval & A confidence interval for a percentage. \\
