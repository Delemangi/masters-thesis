\section*{Abbreviations and terminology}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abbreviations and terminology}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.19\linewidth}p{0.75\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Term & Meaning in this thesis \\
\midrule
API & Application programming interface. \\
BFF & Backend for frontend; the web application's server-side adapter. \\
CI & Confidence interval; the reported paired intervals condition on saved AI labels. \\
FAQ & Frequently asked question and its stored answer. \\
GPU & Graphics processing unit used for embedding, reranking or local inference. \\
LLM & Large language model. \\
MCP & Model Context Protocol, used by the application for structured tool access. \\
RAG & Retrieval-augmented generation. \\
G0/G1 & Shared FAQ/document score floors, with fallback off/on. \\
S0/S1 & Separate FAQ/document score floors, with fallback off/on. \\
Assistance & The combined retained supported-answer or useful-routing outcome. It is an AI-assessed endpoint, not verified factual accuracy. \\
Reference & The AI-produced description of required facts, evidence, action and uncertainty used for assessment. \\
Replay & Re-execution of production retrieval and prompt logic using captured candidates and controlled policy changes. \\
Material claim & A consequential statement about matters such as eligibility, fees, deadlines, procedure or an institutional action. \\
Fallback & Retaining the best candidate when neither corpus has a candidate surviving its score floor; distinct from reranker-error recovery. \\
