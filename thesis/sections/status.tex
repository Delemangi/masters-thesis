\section*{Status of this manuscript}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Status of this manuscript}

This is the first substantial working version of the full master's thesis. It is a separate document from the short paper and is not presented as a final submission. The chapters describe the inspected implementation, available datasets, completed experiments and recorded diagnostic analyses. Their numerical results come from retained research artifacts; expanding the manuscript has not added new inference runs or independent human validation.

The research focus is system effectiveness: supported and sufficiently complete answers, appropriate clarification or referral, and the limitations that remain. Staff-time savings, avoided emails and other administrative metrics are outside scope. The architecture explains the system and the tested path. The main experiment covers production retrieval and prompt construction with controlled generation, rather than the entire live application.

Before final submission, the manuscript needs supervisory review, any required institutional formatting decisions and a final editorial pass. Stronger source and assessor validation would improve the evidence. Full conversation and live-tool evaluation are proposed extensions and are never reported here as completed experiments. No publication, acknowledgement, approval or human-review claim has been inherited from the example theses used to understand the template.

The supplied correspondence and telemetry were analyzed with permission. Raw questions, answers and review records remain in restricted local research files. The manuscript uses aggregates and a paraphrased example; its source bundle contains no private datasets, credentials or copies of previous theses. The retained code, manifests and evidence support local inspection, but a manuscript-only bundle does not independently reproduce the private-data experiment.
