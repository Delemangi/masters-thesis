\section*{Scope and data use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Scope and data use}

The research focus is system effectiveness: supported and sufficiently complete answers, appropriate clarification or referral, and the limitations that remain. Staff-time savings, avoided emails and other administrative metrics are outside scope. The architecture explains the system and the tested path. The main experiment covers production retrieval and prompt construction with controlled generation, rather than the entire live application. Full conversation and live-tool evaluation are proposed extensions and are not reported as completed experiments.

The supplied correspondence and telemetry were analyzed with permission. Raw questions, answers and review records remain in restricted local research files. The manuscript uses aggregates and a paraphrased example; its source bundle contains no private datasets, credentials or copies of previous theses. The retained code, manifests and evidence support local inspection, but a manuscript-only bundle does not independently reproduce the private-data experiment.
