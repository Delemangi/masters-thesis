Retrieval-augmented generation uses external evidence to generate an answer~\cite{lewis2020rag}. Dense passage retrieval compares learned numerical representations of questions and passages to find candidates~\cite{karpukhin2020dpr}. A cross-encoder can then score each question and passage together~\cite{nogueira2019bertrerank}. M3-Embedding supports multiple languages, retrieval methods and text lengths~\cite{chen2024m3}. We use these methods in the architecture, but their published benchmark results do not measure performance on FCSE student questions.

MIRACL covers 18 languages~\cite{zhang2023miracl}, and NoMIRACL studies multilingual relevance assessment for RAG~\cite{thakur2024nomiracl}. Research on Macedonian models and corpora also supports the need for language-specific evaluation~\cite{krsteski2025macedonian}. Supporting a language does not establish how well a model handles this institution's documents and questions. Our evaluation includes Macedonian script checks and reports email and chatbot samples separately because they differ in content as well as script.

RAGAs provides automated measures of RAG quality~\cite{es2024ragas}, while research on LLM judges identifies assessment biases~\cite{zheng2023judge}. Research on answering unfamiliar types of questions examines when a model should decline to answer~\cite{kamath2020selective}. Irrelevant context can distract arithmetic reasoning~\cite{shi2023distracted}. Claim-verification research also finds that models use synthetic evidence differently from evidence found by retrieval~\cite{hagstrom2025reality}. These findings do not directly estimate the effect of the chatbot's retrieval rules.

Marcel~\cite{trienes2025marcel} is a closely related university assistant. It combines RAG, evaluation on real emails and separate observations from deployment. We test minimum-score and fallback effects within the chatbot's deployed retrieval code. We also separate answers from routing and check how references and judge choice affect the results. This is evidence about one implementation, not a new general RAG method or a claim of superiority to Marcel.
